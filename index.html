New here?

Your first Sunday, step by step.

No guesswork. Here's how the morning goes — and what we'll never ask of you.

  1. Let us know you're coming

    Tell us a little about yourself in a quick form, and a friendly face will be waiting at the door to show you around.

  2. Check in the kids, grab a coffee

    Kids check-in opens 20 minutes early at the Kids Wing desk, so your little ones are settled before things start.

  3. Worship, then stay a while

    About 70 minutes of live music and a practical message from the Bible. The conversations after are where friendships start.

  • Come as you are — jeans are fine
  • No one will single you out
  • You're not expected to give
